Privacy policy

Editoo app (Android, ru.agentika.aimontage)

Version of 24 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible for your data

1.1. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Editoo app (the "App") processes, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

1.2. The data controller is the App's developer, Aleksei Korol, Türkiye (the "Developer", "we"). On Google Play the App is published by the developer account Editoo.

1.3. Privacy questions, deletion requests and complaints: info@agentika.info.

1.4. This Policy follows the Google Play User Data policy, Law No. 6698 of the Republic of Türkiye on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) and Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

2. In short

  • There is no account in the App: we do not ask for your name, phone number or e-mail, we do not collect location or contacts, we show no ads and we do not sell data.
  • Your videos, photos, music, voice notes, chats with the editor, projects and API keys are stored on your phone.
  • To edit, the App sends the necessary material directly from your phone to the AI services you connected with your own key. Your videos, voice and texts do not pass through the Developer's servers. The App contains no Developer keys: all requests use your keys and are paid by you to those services.
  • Anonymous edit statistics are sent to the Developer only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

3. What data is processed

3.1. Material you choose yourself: videos, photos, audio (music, voice recordings, voice-over), task texts and briefs, brief attachments (PDFs, documents, images, sample videos). The App only accesses the files you select. Videos and recordings may contain people's images and voices. The App does not use them to identify anyone.

3.2. Voice messages: the microphone is used only while you hold or tap the record button.

3.3. Chat with the editor in a project, edit plans and the work log (for troubleshooting) are stored on your phone.

3.4. API keys for AI services are stored on your phone in encrypted Android storage and are sent only to the service they belong to.

3.5. Technical data: a random install ID (not linked to your Google account, phone or keys) and the App version. The SIM/network country and the phone language are checked on the device to suggest a suitable way to connect AI services; they are not sent anywhere.

3.6. Anonymous edit statistics (only with consent): correction-type labels ("pace", "music", "captions", etc.), 1–3 sentences generalising what you did not like about the result — without the text of your messages, names, phone numbers, e-mails, links, brands, numbers or quotes (the App checks this before sending and, if in doubt, sends the labels only), project type, date (day), App version and random install ID.

3.7. Report on AI output (the "Report" button): complaint type, what it is about (video, track, cover), project type, date, App version and install ID. The App also opens your e-mail app with a message to info@agentika.info containing the reason, your comment (if any) and the App version. You send this e-mail yourself, so we receive your e-mail address.

3.8. Face detection for blurring (the "hide faces" feature) runs on your phone using the built-in Google ML Kit library; face images are not sent anywhere. ML Kit may send Google anonymous technical information about its operation (device model, versions, a per-installation ID, error codes, performance metrics) for diagnostics and usage analytics.

3.9. The App contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services (such as Firebase or Crashlytics).

4. Who receives data and why

Data is sent only to the services needed for the operation you run, and only if you connected that service with your key:

  • Google Gemini (Google LLC) — videos (compressed), frames and brief attachments, to analyse footage, pick the best moments, check the finished video and generate images;
  • Anthropic Claude (Anthropic PBC) — a text description of the footage, speech transcript, task, brief and project chat, to build the edit plan;
  • ProxyAPI (proxyapi.ru) — if you chose to pay via ProxyAPI instead of your own Google and Anthropic keys: the same data as for Gemini and Claude goes through ProxyAPI, which forwards it to Google and Anthropic;
  • ElevenLabs — audio for timed transcription and voice cleanup, texts for voice-over and dubbing, prompts for music and sound generation;
  • Deepgram — audio and voice messages for transcription;
  • Pixabay — search words for stock videos and photos;
  • Higgsfield — prompts and, if needed, frames for generated B-roll — only after you confirm;
  • Shotstack — video clips and the plan for cloud rendering — only after you confirm;
  • Submagic — video for captions by an external service — only on your explicit request;
  • Yandex Disk — the App only downloads editing-rule updates from a public folder; no user data is sent;
  • Google (ML Kit) — technical information about the library (section 3.8);
  • the Developer — anonymous statistics (section 3.6, with consent only), reports on AI output (section 3.7) and e-mails you send us.

These services process data under their own terms and privacy policies; please read them before connecting a service. They are usually located outside your country (including outside Russia), so the transfer may be cross-border.

We do not sell data or share it with advertising networks.

5. Purposes and legal basis

5.1. Purposes: editing videos for your task; transcription and captions; voice-over, dubbing and translation; picking and generating music, sounds, B-roll and images; handling reports; improving editing quality from anonymous statistics.

5.2. Legal basis: your consent (the Consent to personal data processing and a separate checkbox for statistics) and performance of the Terms of Use.

6. Retention and deletion

6.1. On your phone: projects, media, chats and logs are kept while the project exists in the App. To delete a project: long-press it on the home screen → Delete. Finished videos and images saved to the gallery (Movies/AI-Montage, Pictures/AI-Montage) are deleted in the gallery. Uninstalling the App erases all its data on the phone except what was saved to the gallery. Cloud backup of App data to Google is disabled.

6.2. With the Developer: anonymous statistics, reports and e-mails are kept while the related project exists in the App, or until you ask us to delete them. To delete statistics, e-mail info@agentika.info with your install ID (shown on the "Support" screen: tap the version number in your profile 7 times); e-mails and reports are deleted on request from the address you wrote from. If you uninstall the App, its install ID is gone, and a new installation gets a new ID.

6.3. With third-party services: retention is set by their rules (for example, files uploaded to Google Gemini are deleted by the service automatically after a limited time). You can delete data there in your account with that service or by contacting it. We have no access to your accounts with these services.

7. Your rights

You can: find out what data about you is processed; ask for it to be corrected or deleted; withdraw consent to statistics (Profile → Privacy and data — turn off the switch) or any consent by writing to info@agentika.info; complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Türkiye — the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK; in Russia — Roskomnadzor). The App cannot work without consent to personal data processing: uninstall it or write to us.

8. Security

Keys are kept in encrypted Android storage (Android Keystore, AES-256). All connections to services and to the Developer's server use HTTPS. Do not share your keys with anyone.

9. Children

The App is intended for people aged 18 and over (the AI services it relies on require this) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process children's data. If you learn that a child has sent us data, write to info@agentika.info and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy; the version date is shown at the top. The current version is available in the App (Profile → Documents & about) and on the website. We will announce material changes in the App.
